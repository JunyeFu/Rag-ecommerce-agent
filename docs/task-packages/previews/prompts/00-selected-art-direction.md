# 已确认美术方向：编辑实验室

- 用户选择：三案中的第 2 张。
- 目标尺寸：Android `390 × 844`，只输出 App 内容。
- 参考图：`../art-direction/selected-editorial-tech.png`

## 视觉提示词

Create a production-quality Android native RAG shopping Agent UI using a kinetic editorial-tech visual system. Use oversized Chinese typography, asymmetric modular grids, technical annotation lines, subtle engineering grids, tactile halftone grain, dramatically cropped 3D fictional product imagery, large fit-score numerals and a graphic process rail. Use bone `#F4F0E7`, near-black `#151713`, electric acid-lime `#C7FF45`, emerald `#087A46`, signal coral `#FF5F50` and cool steel `#BCC6C7`. Keep the result sophisticated, experimental and implementable in Jetpack Compose rather than decorative concept art.

Preserve the Agent-first product rules: one Shopping Mission, grounded evidence, visible risks, public execution stages only, and three primary destinations—任务、决策、我的. Do not add marketplace banners, category grids, promotional clutter, cart, order, payment, real brand marks or chain of thought. Use no more than two fonts, practical touch targets and readable body text.

## 可实现美术元素

- 超大标题与非对称网格形成主视觉，而不是增加商城广告。
- 产品图采用真实 PNG/WebP 切图和受控裁剪，不使用代码假画产品。
- Compose Canvas 绘制工程网格、标注线、匹配环和轻量数据图形。
- 酸性荧光绿仅用于当前进度、匹配分数和主操作；珊瑚色仅用于风险。
- 页面切换和 Agent 进度使用短时位移、数字滚动和路径推进；支持减少动态效果。
